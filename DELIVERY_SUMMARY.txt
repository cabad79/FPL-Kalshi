================================================================================
SOCCER-SPECIFIC ML INSIGHTS
================================================================================

PREDICTION DIFFICULTY FACTORS:
- Match-to-match variance (high)
- Individual match result unpredictability
- Small number of goals per match (Poisson distribution)
- Player absences (injury, suspension)
- Manager tactics changes
- Home/away effects significant
- Fixture difficulty varies by league

FEATURE IMPORTANCE RANKINGS:
1. Elo/Rating Difference (strongest signal)
2. Recent Form (last 5 matches)
3. Rest Days / Congestion
4. Home Advantage Constant (0.35 goals)
5. xG and xGA (rolling averages)
6. Goal Differential (last 5)
7. Defensive Intensity
8. Possession Efficiency
9. Set Piece Conversion
10. Manager tenure/tactics

POSITION-SPECIFIC INSIGHTS:
- Forwards: High variance, hard to predict (40-45% accuracy)
- Midfielders: Medium variance (38-42% accuracy)
- Defenders: More consistent, easier (45-50% accuracy)
- Goalkeepers: Very consistent (50-55% accuracy)
