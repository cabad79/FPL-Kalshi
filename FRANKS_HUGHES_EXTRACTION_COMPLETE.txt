================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS FROM FRANKS & HUGHES
================================================================================

1. METRICS ARE CONTEXTUAL
   - High possession ≠ good performance
   - xG is predictive, not deterministic
   - Possession efficiency = xG ÷ possession %

2. TACTICS MATTER MORE THAN TALENT
   - Formation fit crucial for player performance
   - Tactical matchup predicts outcome better than individual ratings
   - Team organization reduces individual skill variance

3. TRANSITION SPEED IS CRITICAL
   - Counter-attack success depends on recovery speed
   - Average transition time: 8-10 seconds
   - Quick transitions (2-3 passes) most effective

4. PRESSING MODELS ARE UNSUSTAINABLE LONG-TERM
   - High-press fatigue builds over 60 minutes
   - Success rate declines from 25% to 18% in second half
   - Risk: Exposed backline when press fails

5. SET-PIECES ARE SPECIALIZED SKILLS
   - Set-piece threat independent from open-play
   - ~15% of goals come from set-pieces
   - Defensive set-piece organization critical

6. PLAYER ROLES VARY BY TEAM
   - Same player = different performance in different systems
   - Position metrics vary ±20% based on team tactical role
   - Peer comparison must account for tactical context

7. HOME ADVANTAGE IS QUANTIFIABLE
   - +10-20% xG adjustment
   - Away performance degrades 5-15%
   - Sleep deprivation and fatigue compound effect
